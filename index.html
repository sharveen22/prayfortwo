Pray for Two

A daily prayer app for Christian couples: pray for each other, pray together, and keep a Book of Remembrance of what God has done.

Common questions

How do I connect with my partner? During setup you’ll get an invite code. Your partner installs Pray for Two, taps “My partner invited me” and enters the code.

Does my partner need to subscribe too? No. One subscription covers you both.

How do I cancel? Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pray for Two.

Can my partner read my private prayers? No. Private prayers never leave your phone; your partner only sees that you prayed.
