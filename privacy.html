Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Pray for Two is built so that your prayers stay between you, your partner and God. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and we never show ads.

What we store

  • Your account. An anonymous account is created automatically; we don’t ask for your email or phone number. We store the first name and avatar you choose so your partner sees them.
  • Prayers you share. When you mark a prayer as shared, what you wrote (what you’re thankful for and what you prayed for) is stored so your partner can read it.
  • Private prayers. Only the fact that you prayed, and when, is stored — so your shared streak counts. The words never leave your iPhone.
  • Notifications. A device token so we can tell your partner when you’ve prayed.
  • Subscription. Your plan and its renewal date, so one subscription can cover you both. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to keep subscription records.

Spoken prayers

If you speak your prayer notes, they’re turned into text on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device speech recognition. No audio is sent to us.

Where it’s kept

Data is stored with Supabase (United States) and protected so that only you and your partner can read your couple’s data.

Your choices

You can disconnect from your partner at any time in the app. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email sharveenkumar@gmail.com and we’ll delete it within 30 days.

Children

Pray for Two isn’t directed to children under 13.
